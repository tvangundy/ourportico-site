Family journey · Files · Photos

Family photos, shared from home

After beach week, the Reyes family had 600 photos spread across four phones. Grandma wanted to see all of them, and Aunt Lucia wanted to add hers. Here is how they put them in one Portico Files folder on their own Edge Agent and shared it with exactly those people.

  • You'll needAn Edge Agent on an always-on computer at home, plus Portico Mobile
  • AppsPortico Files (Photos view, Portico Photos)
  • TimeAbout 15 minutes, plus upload time

What you'll end up with

  • A "Family Photos" folder kept on your agent at home, not on someone else's cloud.
  • Grandparents who can browse and swipe through the photos but can't change them.
  • One relative who can also upload photos.

  1. Have an Edge Agent at home

    Files are kept on your own computer, so sharing folders needs an Edge Agent. A Cloud Agent covers lists only.

    1. Pick an always-on computer: an old Mac mini, a Linux box, or a Raspberry Pi with a big drive.
    2. Follow Install Portico Edge.
    3. Open the Edge Agent console and sign in.

    Keep it awake: if that computer sleeps, nobody can reach the photos.

  2. Add a Portico Files folder

    A folder you add here becomes a "Thing" you can share with people.

    1. In the Things panel, tap + (Add Thing).
    2. Set Type to Folder (Portico Files).
    3. Name: Family Photos. Folder name: photos. Tap Check folder. The folder is created if it doesn't exist yet.
    4. Leave Users read-only checked. Guests can look but not change anything until you say so.
    5. Tap Save.

  3. Upload the photos

    Organize by event so the photo wall is easy to browse.

    1. Tap the Family Photos card in Things to open it in Portico Files.
    2. Tap New folder and name it 2026 Beach week.
    3. Open that folder and tap Upload to pick photos, or drag them onto the page from a computer.

  4. Invite Grandma and Grandpa

    An invitation decides exactly what someone can reach. Here, that's this one folder.

    1. In the Invitations panel, tap + (New invite).
    2. Set Invitation type to Things.
    3. Invite name: Grandma's iPad. Add her email in To email (optional).
    4. Under Share things, tick Family Photos.
    5. Tap Create, then send the link by text or email, or show the QR code.

    Leave Connection mode on Portico Relay. You don't need to change anything on your router.

  5. Grandma swipes through beach week

    What she sees after she taps Accept.

    1. She opens the link or scans the QR with her Camera, signs in, and taps Accept in Portico Mobile.
    2. She opens Family Photos and switches the view from Tree to Photos to get a thumbnail grid.
    3. Portico Photos opens a full-screen photo wall. Tap a photo, then swipe for the next one.
    4. She sees a Read-only badge, and there's no upload button for her.

  6. Let Aunt Lucia add her photos

    Give write access one person at a time.

    1. Invite Lucia the same way as in step 4.
    2. Once she has accepted, open Family Photos and tap the Users pill.
    3. Next to Lucia, switch Read-only to Can write.
    4. She now sees Upload and New folder in that folder.

  7. Optional: share one album, watermarked

    Share just one album with a friend, and put their name on every photo they see.

    1. Select the 2026 Beach week folder and tap Share as Thing in the selection bar. Give it a name.
    2. Invite the friend with that new Thing ticked. They see only that album.
    3. Select photos and turn Watermark: On. View-only guests see their own email on the photo and get a View only badge instead of Download.
